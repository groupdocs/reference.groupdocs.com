### Remarks

The handler is called for resources referenced by a template document (for example, images of an HTML template, linked images and INCLUDEPICTURE fields of a Word template) and for URIs that template expressions of `image` and `doc` tags evaluate to.

The handler is called on the thread that assembles a document.
