### Remarks

A preview is a set of images, one per page of a document: a page of a word processing document, a printed page of a spreadsheet, a slide of a presentation, or a page of an email message rendered as a document.

For every previewed page, [`CreatePageStream`](./createpagestream) is called to get a stream to write the page image to. After the image is written, [`ReleasePageStream`](./releasepagestream) is called, if specified, so that the stream can be closed or processed. Streams are not closed by GroupDocs.Assembly.
